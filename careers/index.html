Why Signia

Nursing Work That Uses Your Judgement

Triage is clinical decision-making, not call handling. You bring the assessment skills; we give you the structure, the pathways, policies and procedures, and the support to use them.

Practice At The Top Of Your License

Every call is a real assessment with a real decision attached. No scripts, no message-taking.

A Team That Has Your Back

Experienced senior living nurses, structured guidance, and colleagues who understand the work.

Competitive Compensation

Pay built on RN wage economics, plus a schedule designed around real life.

Room To Grow

Ongoing clinical development and opportunities to shape how our triage program evolves.

Work That Matters After Dark

The hours when residents are most vulnerable are the hours where your expertise counts most.

Communities You Know

You'll be supporting senior living communities and the caregivers who staff them.
