The case for triage

Why RN Triage Matters

Six things change the moment a licensed RN picks up instead of an answering machine.

I

Protects Resident & Client Safety

Residents and clients rely on timely, accurate decisions, whether or not a nurse is in the building. Triage ensures issues are escalated quickly, non-emergent concerns are managed safely on site, and every situation is evaluated through an experienced clinical lens.

II

Reduces Unnecessary ER Transfers

Many transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious. Our RNs help staff distinguish true emergencies from issues that can be safely monitored, reducing stress, cost and hospital exposure.

III

Supports Staff Confidence

Caregivers and direct support staff often work with limited clinical backup. Triage gives them a calm, experienced RN who validates their assessment and guides next steps, reducing anxiety and improving retention.

IV

Improves Family Communication

Families want clear, timely updates on their loved ones. Our team provides a clinical voice they can trust, helping families understand what's happening and what comes next.

V

Keeps Documentation Complete And Compliant

Every call is documented in real time, with no lapses between shifts, and in line with state regulatory requirements for your setting. Decisions follow your policies, procedures and escalation pathways on every call.

VI

Protects Leadership Time

With triage in place, leadership is contacted only when clinically appropriate. Your nurses can focus on their day, leadership's nights and weekends are interrupted less, and mornings start with a complete record instead of unanswered questions.

Frequently Asked Questions

If the answer you need isn't here, call (763) 308-3282 and ask us directly.

What is RN triage?

RN triage gives your staff a registered nurse to call whenever a resident's or client's condition changes and no nurse is on site. The RN assesses the person's symptoms, determines urgency, gives staff clear step-by-step directions, recommends a safe next action and documents the call in real time for leadership. It is clinical decision support, not an answering service that takes a message.

Do you offer daytime triage or only after hours?

Both. Signia Solutions now offers daytime RN triage, for when your nurse is off site, in meetings or trainings, covering several buildings or busy with another resident or client, alongside after-hours triage for nights, weekends and holidays. Daytime coverage can be switched on and off as your facility needs it, including intermittently, and costs a fraction of an RN's hourly wage.

How is Signia Solutions different from an answering service or call center?

An answering service records a message and passes it along. Signia's calls are answered by an experienced registered nurse who assesses the situation, applies evidence-based clinical guidance and your community's own policies, procedures and escalation pathways, and gives your staff a specific next step. Every call is documented and sent to leadership.

Does nurse triage reduce unnecessary ER transfers?

That is the intent. Many transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious rather than because the person needs an emergency department. An on-call RN helps staff distinguish true emergencies from concerns that can be safely monitored on site, which reduces stress, cost and hospital exposure for residents and clients.

Which settings does Signia Solutions serve?

Senior living communities, including independent living with clinical oversight, assisted living and memory care, and customized HCBS settings, including group homes and specialized living. The model works for a single home or community as well as an operator running many buildings across markets.

What services does Signia Solutions offer?

RN triage, during the day and after hours. Experienced registered nurses answer your staff's calls, give clear directions and document every call in real time, in line with state regulations. You can choose daytime coverage, after-hours coverage (nights, weekends and holidays), or both.

How does Signia Solutions pricing work?

After-hours triage is a flat monthly fee aligned to your census, built from RN wage economics. That gives you a predictable monthly cost rather than per-call billing that spikes in a hard month. Daytime triage costs a fraction of an RN's hourly wage, and you only pay for the days you switch it on. Contact us for a quote for your community.

Do your nurses follow our escalation policies?

Yes. We operate as an extension of your team, following your policies, procedures and escalation pathways. Your on-call tree, your notification thresholds and your clinical standards are built into the workflow before the first call, so decisions align with how your community already works.

What happens after a call is completed?

Every call is documented and sent to leadership for transparency and follow-through. If the situation requires leadership involvement or emergency services, the RN initiates the appropriate pathway under your policies and procedures at the time of the call rather than waiting for morning.
