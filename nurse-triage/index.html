The case for triage

Why After-Hours Triage Matters

Six things change the night a licensed RN picks up instead of an answering machine.

I

Protects Resident Safety

Residents rely on timely, accurate decisions — especially after hours. Triage ensures issues are escalated quickly, non-emergent concerns are managed safely on site, and every situation is evaluated through an experienced clinical lens.

II

Reduces Unnecessary ER Transfers

Many after-hours transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious. Our on-call RNs help staff distinguish true emergencies from issues that can be safely monitored — reducing stress, cost and hospital exposure.

III

Supports Staff Confidence

After-hours teams often work with limited clinical backup. Triage gives them a calm, experienced RN who validates their assessment and guides next steps, reducing anxiety and improving retention.

IV

Improves Family Communication

Families want clear, timely updates on their loved ones — reassurance and clarity. Our team provides a clinical voice they can trust, helping families understand what's happening and what comes next.

V

Creates Consistent Decision-Making

Every community has its own workflows and expectations. Triage ensures decisions align with your policies, procedures, escalation pathways and clinical standards — night after night.

VI

Protects Leadership Time

With triage in place, leadership is contacted only when clinically appropriate. You can truly reclaim your nights and weekends. Overnight disruptions decrease, documentation is complete, and mornings start with clarity — not unanswered questions.

The workflow

How Signia Fits Into Your Workflow

Nothing about your operation has to change. We plug into the pathways, policies and procedures your team already follows and run them the same way, every night.

Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

If the answer you need isn't here, call (763) 308-3282 and ask us directly.

What is after-hours nurse triage for senior living?

After-hours nurse triage gives your caregivers a registered nurse to call when the building quiets and on-site clinical leadership has gone home. The RN assesses the resident's symptoms, determines urgency, guides staff through a structured evaluation, recommends a safe next action and documents the call for leadership. It is clinical decision support, not an answering service that takes a message.

How is Signia Solutions different from an answering service or call center?

An answering service records a message and passes it along. Signia's calls are answered by an experienced registered nurse who assesses the situation, applies evidence-based clinical guidance and your community's own policies, procedures and escalation pathways, and gives your caregiver a specific next step. Every call is documented and sent to leadership.

Does nurse triage reduce unnecessary ER transfers?

That is the intent. Many after-hours transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious rather than because the resident needs an emergency department. An on-call RN helps staff distinguish true emergencies from concerns that can be safely monitored on site, which reduces stress, cost and hospital exposure for the resident.

Which communities does Signia Solutions serve?

Assisted living, memory care, independent living with clinical oversight, multi-building campuses, and regional or enterprise operators. The model works for a single community as well as an operator running many buildings across markets.

How does Signia Solutions pricing work?

Pricing is a flat monthly fee aligned to your census, built from RN wage economics. That gives you a predictable monthly cost rather than per-call billing that spikes in a hard month.

Do your nurses follow our escalation policies?

Yes. We operate as an extension of your team, following your policies, procedures and escalation pathways. Your on-call tree, your notification thresholds and your clinical standards are built into the workflow before the first call, so decisions align with how your community already works.

What happens after a call is completed?

Every call is documented and sent to leadership for transparency and follow-through. If the situation requires leadership involvement or emergency services, the RN initiates the appropriate pathway under your policies and procedures at the time of the call rather than waiting for morning.
