Accessibility Statement

We build for the people who use this site at 2 a.m. on a phone. That means it has to work for everyone.

Our Commitment

Signia Solutions aims to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA across signiasolutions.com. Accessibility is treated as part of building the site, not a pass we make afterwards.

What We've Done

  • Semantic HTML landmarks and a logical heading order on every page.
  • A "skip to main content" link as the first keyboard stop.
  • Visible focus indicators on every interactive element.
  • Full keyboard operability, including the mobile menu (Escape closes it).
  • Text and interface colors checked against WCAG contrast minimums.
  • Meaningful alternative text on informative images; decorative graphics hidden from assistive technology.
  • Text that scales — pinch-zoom is never disabled, and layouts reflow down to 320 px.
  • Motion honours the operating-system "reduce motion" setting.
  • Form fields with real, programmatically associated labels and clear error messaging.

Known Limitations

Any third-party content added later — an embedded map, a video player or a hosted job board — may not fully meet our standard. Where that happens we will provide an accessible alternative and note it here.
