About us

We exist to close the after-hours gap

Signia Solutions provides calm, clinically sound after-hours RN triage for senior living communities. We were created to close the gap that occurs when the building quiets, staffing thins, and critical decisions still need to be made.

Our story

Minnesota nurses, supporting Minnesota caregivers

Our Minnesota-based nurses support caregivers with real-time clinical guidance so residents receive safe, timely care — every night.

Regardless of size, staffing, or acuity, we strive to elevate resident safety, empower frontline caregivers, and bring consistency to every decision made in the moments that matter most.

Our core values

The principles that guide every call

Every call, every shift, every night.

Clinical excellence

Our RNs rely on evidence-based practice and deep senior living experience to guide every decision. Resident safety is the foundation of our work.

Calm and clarity

After-hours moments can feel uncertain. We bring steadiness, structured assessment and clear communication to help staff feel supported.

Compassion

We honor the humanity of every resident and the dedication of every caregiver. Empathy and respect shape every interaction.

Consistency & reliability

Communities depend on us to show up the same way every night — responsive, steady and clinically sound.

Partnership

We operate as an extension of your team, following your policies and escalation pathways to ensure seamless alignment.

Integrity & transparency

Clear documentation, honest communication and professional accountability guide everything we do.
