About us

We Exist To Close The Clinical Support Gap

Signia Solutions provides experienced RN triage, during the day and after hours, for senior living communities and customized HCBS settings. We were created to close the gap that opens whenever a nurse isn't on site and critical decisions still need to be made.

Our story

Who We Are

Signia Solutions is a clinical triage partner for senior living communities and customized HCBS providers. Our RNs take your staff's calls during the day, when your nurse is off site or busy, and after hours, on nights, weekends and holidays.

Our RN-led team combines clinical expertise with modern, operator-friendly workflows designed to reduce friction and improve outcomes. We specialize in reliable, compassionate clinical triage, with clear directions to staff and real-time documentation that meets state requirements.

Our mission is simple: deliver dependable clinical support that strengthens care consistency, gives respite to your nurses, and empowers your staff to focus on what matters most: the people you serve.
