The case for triage

Why after-hours triage matters

Six things change the night a licensed RN picks up instead of an answering machine.

I

Protects resident safety

Residents rely on timely, accurate decisions — especially after hours. Triage ensures issues are escalated quickly, non-emergent concerns are managed safely on site, and every situation is evaluated through an experienced clinical lens.

II

Reduces unnecessary ER transfers

Many after-hours transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious. Our on-call RNs help staff distinguish true emergencies from issues that can be safely monitored — reducing stress, cost and hospital exposure.

III

Supports staff confidence

After-hours teams often work with limited clinical backup. Triage gives them a calm, experienced RN who validates their assessment and guides next steps, reducing anxiety and improving retention.

IV

Improves family communication

Families want clear, timely updates on their loved ones — reassurance and clarity. Our team provides a clinical voice they can trust, helping families understand what's happening and what comes next.

V

Creates consistent decision-making

Every community has its own workflows and expectations. Triage ensures decisions align with your policies, escalation pathways and clinical standards — night after night.

VI

Protects leadership time

With triage in place, leadership is contacted only when clinically appropriate. You can truly reclaim your nights and weekends. Overnight disruptions decrease, documentation is complete, and mornings start with clarity — not unanswered questions.

The workflow

How Signia fits into your workflow

Nothing about your operation has to change. We plug into the pathways your team already follows and run them the same way, every night.

Map it to our community

  1. Staff call our dedicated line

    Your caregivers reach an experienced RN within seconds.

  2. The RN assesses the situation

    Using structured clinical guidance and your community's specific pathways, policies and procedures, the nurse evaluates urgency and risk.

  3. Clear guidance is provided

    Staff receive step-by-step instructions tailored to the resident's condition.

  4. Documentation is completed

    Every call is documented and sent to leadership for transparency and follow-through.

  5. Escalation happens when needed

    If the situation requires leadership involvement or emergency services, the RN initiates the appropriate pathway.
