The case for triage

Why RN Triage Matters

Six things change the moment a licensed RN picks up instead of an answering machine.

I

Protects Resident & Client Safety

Residents and clients rely on timely, accurate decisions, whether or not a nurse is in the building. Triage ensures issues are escalated quickly, non-emergent concerns are managed safely on site, and every situation is evaluated through an experienced clinical lens.

II

Reduces Unnecessary ER Transfers

Many transfers happen because staff feel uncertain or families feel anxious. Our RNs help staff distinguish true emergencies from issues that can be safely monitored, reducing stress, cost and hospital exposure.

III

Supports Staff Confidence

Caregivers and direct support staff often work with limited clinical backup. Triage gives them a calm, experienced RN who validates their assessment and guides next steps, reducing anxiety and improving retention.

IV

Improves Family Communication

Families want clear, timely updates on their loved ones. Our team provides a clinical voice they can trust, helping families understand what's happening and what comes next.

V

Keeps Documentation Complete And Compliant

Every call is documented in real time, with no lapses between shifts, and in line with state regulatory requirements for your setting. Decisions follow your policies, procedures and escalation pathways on every call.

VI

Protects Leadership Time

With triage in place, leadership is contacted only when clinically appropriate. Your nurses can focus on their day, leadership's nights and weekends are interrupted less, and mornings start with a complete record instead of unanswered questions.

The workflow

How Signia Fits Into Your Workflow

Nothing about your operation has to change. We plug into the pathways, policies and procedures your team already follows and run them the same way on every call, day or night.

  1. Staff Call Our Dedicated Line

    Your staff reach an experienced RN within seconds. A real person answers.

  2. The RN Assesses The Situation

    Using structured clinical guidance and your specific pathways, policies and procedures, the nurse evaluates urgency and risk.

  3. Clear Directions Are Provided

    Staff receive step-by-step instructions tailored to the person's condition.

  4. Documentation Is Completed In Real Time

    Every call is documented as it happens and sent to leadership for transparency and follow-through.

  5. Escalation Happens When Needed

    If the situation requires leadership involvement or emergency services, the RN initiates the appropriate pathway under your policies and procedures.
