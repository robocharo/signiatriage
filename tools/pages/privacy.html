Privacy Policy

How we handle information submitted through signiasolutions.com.

Who We Are

Signia Solutions ("we", "us") provides after-hours registered nurse triage services to senior living communities. Our office is at 10405 6th Ave N, Plymouth, MN 55441. You can reach us at (763) 308-3282 or hi@signiasolutions.com.

What This Policy Covers

This policy covers information collected through this website. It does not cover protected health information (PHI) handled under our service agreements with partner communities. That information is governed by those agreements, by HIPAA and by any applicable business associate agreement — not by this page.

Information We Collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit the contact or careers form we collect what you type into it — for example your name, email address, phone number, organization, care setting, approximate census, any message you write, and a resume if you attach one.
  • Information collected automatically. Our hosting provider records standard server request data such as IP address, browser user agent, referring page and timestamp. This is used for security and reliability.

Please Do Not Send Us Health Information Here

The forms on this site are for business inquiries and employment applications only. Do not include resident names, medical details or any other protected health information in a web form. Partner communities should use the dedicated triage line for clinical matters. If a resident needs immediate medical attention, call 911.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to your inquiry and follow up about services or employment.
  • To evaluate applications for open roles.
  • To keep the site secure, available and working correctly.
  • To meet legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Who We Share Information With

We share information only with service providers who help us operate — for example our website host, our form-handling provider and our email provider — and only to the extent they need it to perform that work. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect the rights and safety of people we serve.

Cookies And Analytics

This site sets no advertising cookies and loads no third-party trackers by default. If analytics is added later, this section will name the provider, describe what is collected and explain how to opt out.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep inquiry and application records only as long as needed for the purpose they were collected for, and to meet legal and recordkeeping obligations. You may ask us to delete yours at any time.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you have submitted through this site. Email hi@signiasolutions.com or call (763) 308-3282 and we will respond promptly. Depending on where you live, additional rights may apply.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS and we apply reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to the information we hold. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so please use the channels above rather than a web form for anything sensitive.

Children

This site is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

Changes To This Policy

If we make material changes we will update this page and revise the date above.
